# BIDMC source facts and scope

Official source: https://physionet.org/content/chfdb/1.0.0/

PhysioNet describes the BIDMC Congestive Heart Failure Database as long-term ECG recordings from 15 subjects with severe congestive heart failure. Each recording is approximately 20 hours, has two ECG signals sampled at 250 samples/second and has `.ecg` annotation files generated by an automated detector which were not manually corrected. The dataset is open access under the Open Data Commons Attribution License v1.0.

**Use in SteadyStep:** evaluate ECG-specific peak detection and conversion from beat intervals to heart rate against annotation-derived reference values. This is aligned with the Hardware PS suggestion that BIDMC can help test heart-rate measurement code.

**Do not use it to:** infer daily weight or foot-to-foot impedance, create real worsening labels that the data does not contain, calibrate foot-to-foot impedance direction, or claim that the full SteadyStep alert system predicts hospitalisation. The ECG waveforms are not platform BCG waveforms. The full alert decision logic must be evaluated separately using the required synthetic longitudinal cohort.

Suggested dataset citation: Baim DS, Colucci WS, Monrad ES, et al. Survival of patients with severe congestive heart failure treated with oral milrinone. *Journal of the American College of Cardiology*. 1986;7(3):661–670. Dataset availability and details: PhysioNet, BIDMC Congestive Heart Failure Database v1.0.0, https://physionet.org/content/chfdb/1.0.0/ .
